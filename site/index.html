How to install

  1. Download the .dmg for your Mac and open it.
  2. Drag Babu Video Player into Applications.
  3. Open it from Applications. The app isn't notarized yet, so macOS blocks the first launch. Use the steps for your macOS version below.
  4. To make it your default player: select a video → Get Info → Open with → Change All.

First launch: "Babu Video Player was blocked"

macOS 15 and later (including macOS 26)

  1. Click Done. Don't click Move to Bin.
  2. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll to the message “Babu Video Player was blocked” and click Open Anyway. Enter your password.

macOS 14 and earlier

  1. Right-click (or Control-click) the app in Applications and choose Open.
  2. Click Open in the dialog.

Terminal alternative

xattr -cr "/Applications/Babu Video Player.app"

You only need to do this once.
