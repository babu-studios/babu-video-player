Play almost any video on your Mac.

A fast, clean, open-source player. Opens in under a second, handles MKV, AVI, HEVC and more, and never asks for your camera or microphone.

Not sure which one? Apple menu → About This Mac. “Chip: Apple M…” means Apple Silicon; “Processor: Intel” means Intel.

Plays everything

MP4, M4V, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, FLV, TS, MPEG, 3GP, HEVC and AV1.

Audio & subtitles

Switch audio tracks and embedded subtitles on the fly.

Remembers where you stopped

Resume each file from its last position, with a recent-files list.

Private by design

Works offline, no telemetry, and only opens files you choose.

Install

  1. Download the .dmg for your Mac and open it.
  2. Drag Babu Video Player into Applications.
  3. First launch: right-click the app and choose Open. The app isn't notarized yet, so macOS asks once.
  4. To make it your default player: select a video → Get Info → Open with → Change All.

Still blocked? Run xattr -cr "/Applications/Babu Video Player.app" in Terminal.
